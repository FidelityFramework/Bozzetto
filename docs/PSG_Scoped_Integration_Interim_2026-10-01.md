   versions. Dormant library growth still changes an unchanged entry ID from
   117 to 684. Equal body counts and byte lengths do not establish zero resend.
2. Author typed artifact reservation, factory-result, residence and ordered
   initialization accounts in Baker. Composer's environment artifact reader
   still depends on analytical edge roles which public revisions omit.
   Header import coverage also needs explicit successful-entry accounting;
   claiming coverage from an executable declaration body would hide the gap.
3. Carry complete demanded fact scopes over sockets, with exact-base atomic
   install, edit-reservation fences, acknowledgements and joined retirement.
   The external generated typed delivery prototype is not this implementation.
4. Exercise actual receiving packets through current proof, passive witnessing,
   selective witness/object reuse and native execution, including newly demanded
   declarations and changed negative lookup support.

No receiver may infer reachability, reconstruct absent source accounts or request
a whole retained graph to compensate for these unfinished contracts. Missing or
foreign bases and unsupported required accounts must produce diagnostics.
